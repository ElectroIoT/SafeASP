External wiring (what you connect by hand)

ConnectionHow
USB-C (J1)Any USB-C cable to a PC: USB-C to USB-C works too, because CC1 and CC2 each have their own 5.1 k pull-down. The board draws about 20 mA plus the target.
10-pin ISP (J2)Standard AVR 10-pin ribbon: 1 MOSI, 2 VCC, 3 NC, 4/6/8/10 GND, 5 RST, 7 SCK, 9 MISO. The box header key stops a reversed plug.
6-pin ISP (J3)Standard AVR 6-pin (Arduino ICSP): 1 MISO, 2 VCC, 3 SCK, 4 MOSI, 5 RST, 6 GND. Use J2 OR J3, not both at once.
Target supply (JP1)Jumper 1-2 = we send 5 V, 2-3 = we send 3.3 V, none = the target has its own supply. The level shifter always follows the voltage on ISP pin 2, so the target pin 2 MUST have power (ours or its own) or the outputs stay off.
NeverNever fit JP1 when the target has its own supply (two supplies fighting). Never feed more than 5.5 V on ISP pin 2.
Slow SCK (JP2)Fit for a target running below 1.5 MHz (for example a new ATmega with CKDIV8 on 1 MHz internal clock), or use avrdude -B 32.
Self-programming (J4)Only to flash the ATmega8A of this board with another programmer. Unplug USB-C first; the other programmer supplies 5 V on pin 2.

Sheet 1: USB-C, V-USB interface, target power

USB-C INPUT

16-pin USB 2.0 Type-C receptacle, one 5.1 k pull-down on EACH of CC1 and CC2 (so C-to-C cables and chargers give 5 V), USBLC6 ESD part on D+ / D-, 500 mA PTC fuse from VBUS to the +5V rail.

V-USB LINE INTERFACE

The classic USBasp low-speed USB interface: 68 R series resistors, 3.6 V zener clamps (the ATmega8A runs at 5 V) and the 2.2 k pull-up on D- that tells the PC this is a low-speed device.

TARGET POWER (5 V / 3.3 V / OFF)

AP2112K 3.3 V LDO. Jumper JP1 picks what the board sends to the target: 5 V (1-2), 3.3 V (2-3) or nothing (no jumper, target has its own supply). A 200 mA PTC fuse limits a shorted target; yellow LED = we are powering the target.

Sheet 2: ATmega8A core

ATMEGA8A CORE

ATmega8A-AU at 5 V with a 12 MHz crystal, running the unchanged USBasp firmware. Red LED (PC1) = programming, green LED (PC0) = USB ready. J4 = 6-pin header to flash this ATmega8A itself; JP2 = slow SCK jumper on PC2.

Sheet 3: Level shifter and ISP headers

LEVEL SHIFTER 1.8 - 5.5 V

74LVC8T245 drives MOSI, SCK and RST to the target at the TARGET's voltage (VTGT); 74LVC1T45 brings MISO back to 5 V. The red-LED line (PC1, low only while programming) enables the outputs, so the target pins are released (Hi-Z) the rest of the time.

ISP HEADERS

Standard AVR 10-pin box header and 6-pin header, both on the shifted target nets. Pin 2 (VCC) is the target supply sense, and the supply output when JP1 is fitted.
